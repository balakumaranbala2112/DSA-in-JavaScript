# **Before Every Problem**

Follow this checklist **before writing code**.

Step 1  
Read the problem carefully.

↓

Step 2  
Ask:  
"Do I need to visit every element?"

↓

Step 3  
If YES

Pattern \= Traversal

↓

Step 4  
Ask:  
"What information do I need to remember?"

↓

Step 5  
Write the algorithm in English.

↓

Step 6  
Write the code.

↓

Step 7  
Dry run it.

↓

Step 8  
Analyze Time & Space Complexity.

This is the workflow you should use for every array problem.
